All calendar buckets are **Europe/Paris** local time; `posted_at` in the snapshot
is UTC.

`timeline-weekly.csv`, `timeline-candidates.csv`.

Three things move together as the campaign heats up:

   framing the idea doc anticipated becomes the dominant cheap format.

## Method notes and limits

- **Top-N sample.** These are the most-engaged videos per candidate, not a
  census. Monthly volume measures *the pull*, and only indirectly the campaign.
- **Overlap.** A video can appear in several candidate files; `unique_videos`
  deduplicates by content id, while the per-candidate table counts each file.
- **Paris time.** A post at 23:43 UTC lands on the next Paris day, which is why
  the first bucket is 2026-08-09 rather than 2026-08-08.
- **Stance columns** come from `candidate-stance.json` (one label per video, from
  the candidate it was scored against). `with_decisive_stance` excludes
  `unclear`, so it is always ≤ videos.
- **Truncated edges.** August is partial (the window opens mid-month) and
  November has 2 days; do not read month-over-month growth from the edges.
